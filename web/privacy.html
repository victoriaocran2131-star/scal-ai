Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Scal AI ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name and email address when you create an account.
  • Health Data: Nutrition logs, food scans, and daily intake data you enter into the app.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and app version for debugging purposes.
  • Images: Food photos you capture or upload for scanning are processed temporarily and not stored permanently.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the Scal AI service.
  • To track your nutrition and health goals.
  • To sync your data across devices.
  • To improve the app and fix bugs.
  • To process food images for identification (images are not stored after processing).

Third-Party Service Providers

We use the following third-party services that may collect or process your data:

  • Firebase (Google Cloud): Authentication, database storage, and analytics. Google's Privacy Policy
  • Google Cloud Vision API: Food image analysis for identification. Images are processed in real-time and not stored by Google.
  • USDA FoodData Central API: Nutrition data lookups for food items.
  • Apple App Store: In-app purchase processing and subscription management.
  • Expo: Push notification delivery and app building services.

These third-party services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud) infrastructure. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties except as described in this policy.

Data Retention

  • Account Data: We retain your account information and nutrition logs for as long as your account is active.
  • Food Images: Images are processed in real-time for identification and are not stored permanently. Temporary processing data is automatically deleted.
  • After Account Deletion: When you delete your account, all your data is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. Your health data stays private and is only accessible by you.

Camera Access

The app uses your camera solely for scanning food items. Camera data is processed on your device and is sent securely to Google Cloud Vision API for identification only. Images are not stored on our servers.

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data and account.
  • Data Portability: Request a copy of your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at support@scalai.app.

Children's Privacy

Scal AI is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@scalai.app.
