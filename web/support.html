Frequently Asked Questions

How does the food scanner work?
Simply point your camera at any food item or scan a barcode. The app uses AI to identify the food and display its nutritional information.
Is my data private?
Yes. Your data is stored securely and is never shared with third parties. You can delete your account and data at any time from the profile screen.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions on your iPhone to manage or cancel your Scal AI subscription.
Can I use the app offline?
Some features like viewing past scan history work offline. Scanning and syncing require an internet connection.
How do I delete my account?
Go to your Profile screen in the app and tap "Delete Account". This will permanently remove all your data.
The scanner isn't working. What should I do?
Make sure you've granted camera permissions to Scal AI in your iPhone Settings. Try restarting the app if the issue persists.
